⚡ Energy
We estimate venue energy from your floor size and event duration — no meter readings needed. BC Hydro is auto-selected for Vancouver BC events (very low impact). Diesel generator has the highest; WA/OR grid is in between.
🥗 Catering
Select the food type that best describes what was served and enter the number of portions (plates served, not headcount — some events serve multiple meals). Rescue or surplus food (e.g. Too Good To Go) has a much lower factor since production emissions are attributed to the original supply chain.
🗑️ Waste
At least one waste field must be filled in — either bag counts below, or the weight override fields if your venue provided a diversion report. A standard garbage bag is assumed at 12 kg. Compost bags are carbon-negative and will reduce your total.
📦 Materials + swag
Enter the number of pages printed for your event. Swag and signage details are collected for our records.
Sustainable event partners
List any sustainable vendors, suppliers, or organizations you partnered with — caterers, waste haulers, transit sponsors, energy providers, and more. Every submission helps us build a shared PNW directory of trusted sustainability partners for future Climate Week organizers.
